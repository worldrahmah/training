# Independent final review prompt / موجّه المراجعة المستقلة

Act as an independent reviewer, not the course's original author. Read owner requirements and the current RC1R10 kit/runtime contract. Inspect the exact submitted package, its SHA-256, validator/import/publication outputs and rehearsal evidence. Do not infer that a reported PASS was executed. Do not rewrite accepted IDs or quietly expand scope.

Review and report findings with severity, exact file/component reference, reproducible failing behavior, learner/Trainer impact and repair/retest requirement:
1. Coverage: objectives, evidence and realistic timing; one accepted milestone at a time; no invented capabilities. Enumerate CORE CAPABILITY GAP items.
2. Structure: exact canonical IDs/settings, schema compatibility, unique stable identifiers, cross-references and complete workflow start/reachability/branches/convergence/terminals/Trainer completion.
3. Pedagogy and roles: usable Participant tasks, facilitation/debrief, Projector legibility, private cues isolated, authored language/direction and accessible alternatives.
4. Assessment: correct/incorrect responses, scoring/participation distinctions, tolerances, result bindings, reveal-safe public feedback and stored report semantics. No answer keys in pre-reveal prose/assets.
5. Packaging: all assets exist locally with rights and checksums; no code/credentials/traversal/duplicates; actual runtime parser and supported import→Draft validation→publication paths executed.
6. Rehearsal: real Trainer, Participant and paired Projector; two users where relevant, restart/reconnect, privacy negatives, EN/AR UI, desktop/tablet/mobile and offline resources. Distinguish PASS/FAIL/SKIPPED/UNRUN.
7. Historical data/version immutability and rollback constraints.

Return a concise readiness decision: accepted for owner rehearsal, changes required, or blocked. List required repairs first, unsupported needs, unresolved assumptions, exact tested package hash and owner visual/classroom acceptance checklist. Never claim FINAL or error-free AI output from parser success alone.

راجع الحزمة بصفتك مراجعًا مستقلًا: الأهداف والأدلة والتوقيت والمكونات والمعرّفات والمسارات والأدوار والخصوصية والتقييم والكشف والأصول والبصمات ومسار الاستيراد والنشر والتجريب الفعلي. ميّز الناجح والفاشل والمتجاوز وغير المنفذ. اذكر شدة كل عيب ومرجعه وخطوات إعادة إنتاجه وإصلاحه واختباره. أعد قرارًا صادقًا وحدودًا معروفة وقائمة قبول لصاحب الدورة، دون الادعاء بالقبول النهائي بناءً على المدقق وحده.
